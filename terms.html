Idonea Health ("Idonea") provides clinical trial pre-screening software to medical practices under a written agreement with each practice. That agreement, together with any Business Associate Agreement, governs the service. These terms summarize it.

Use of the service

Idonea is available only to practices that have signed an agreement with Idonea. It is for pre-screening potential trial participants; it does not diagnose, treat or enroll patients.

Decisions stay with the practice

Idonea suggests and shows its evidence. Every screening decision is made by the practice's qualified staff, who must verify information against the medical record. Values read from documents are not used until a person confirms them.

Data

The practice owns its patient data. Idonea accesses it read-only, only for the practice, as described in the Privacy Policy.

No warranty

The software is provided as is, without warranties beyond those in the practice's written agreement.
