Idonea Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

Idonea Health ("Idonea") makes clinical trial pre-screening software for medical practices. This policy explains how the Idonea app handles patient information it reads from a practice's electronic health record through the ModMed FHIR API.

Whose data, and why

Idonea reads a practice's records only at that practice's request, and only to help its research staff find patients who may qualify for clinical trials. The practice owns its patients' data. Idonea acts as the practice's business associate under HIPAA and handles the data only as the practice's agreement allows.

What the app accesses

Read-only access to the record types the practice approves: patients, conditions, observations, medications, procedures, encounters, allergies, diagnostic reports and documents. The app never writes to or changes the health record.

Where the data goes

Data is exported to software running on a computer the practice owns and controls. Idonea does not keep a copy on its own systems. Where an AI service reads scanned pages, it does so only under a Business Associate Agreement between the practice and that service, and the data is not used to train AI models.

What Idonea never does

Idonea does not sell patient data, use it for advertising, or share it with study sponsors. Sponsors may receive counts only, such as how many patients were screened. A patient's information reaches a sponsor only through the normal informed-consent process run by the practice.

Security

Connections are encrypted. The app signs in with a private key that stays on the practice's computer, and access tokens expire within minutes. Logs record counts and timings, not patient details.
